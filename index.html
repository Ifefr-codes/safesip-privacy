Privacy Policy

Introduction

SafeSip ("we", "our", or "the app") is a smart water tracking app developed by Team AquaMinders, Our Lady's Secondary School Nnobi, Nigeria, as part of the Technovation Girls Programme. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using SafeSip, you agree to the practices described here.

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google's authentication service.
  • Hydration data — Water intake logs, daily goals, streaks, and hydration history that you enter into the app.
  • App usage data — Basic information about how you interact with the app (e.g., reminder preferences, sleep mode settings) to provide a personalised experience.

We do not collect location data, health records, financial information, or any sensitive personal information beyond what is listed above.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your account securely
  • Track and display your daily water intake and hydration streaks
  • Send personalised hydration reminders throughout the day
  • Show your weekly hydration statistics and progress history
  • Improve the app based on general usage patterns

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We use Google Sign-In (provided by Google LLC) for authentication. Google's use of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We may share anonymised, non-identifiable usage data for academic research or programme reporting purposes (e.g., as part of the Technovation Girls Programme), but this will never include your name, email, or personal hydration logs.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google's cloud platform). We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

SafeSip is designed for secondary school students and general users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with their information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by discontinuing use of the app

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of SafeSip after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
